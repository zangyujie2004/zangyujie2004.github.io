Yujie Zang

I am currently a Master’s student in Robotics at the National University of Singapore (NUS), supervised by Prof. Shuicheng Yan. Prior to that, I received my B.Eng. from Shanghai Jiao Tong University (SJTU), where I worked with Prof. Xinyang Tan and graduated as an Outstanding Graduate in 2026.

My research focuses on real-world robotic manipulation, with the goal of building super­human physical intelligence from human priors. I am particularly interested in force and tactile sensing, world modeling, and policy post-training for precise and adaptive robotic skills.
